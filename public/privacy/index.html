Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

HomeFitly is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using HomeFitly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Introduction

HomeFitly is a home maintenance and management application designed to help homeowners stay on top of tasks, warranties, projects, and household organization. We built HomeFitly with a privacy-first mindset: we do not serve advertisements and we never sell your personal data to third parties. Some technical services used by the app process usage, diagnostic or identifier data. Android receipt scanning and, where available, Google Maps features are described in Section 5.

HomeFitly is operated by DataVerse Studios LLC. This policy applies to the HomeFitly apps for iPhone and iPad and for Android, the homefitly.com website, and the associated services. If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us using the contact details in Section 14.

2. Information We Collect

Almost everything HomeFitly stores is information you enter yourself. Technical service information, including the handling of Android receipt scanning and available Google Maps features, is described in Section 5.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the HomeFitly experience. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Provide core app functionality — create and manage your account, display your home profile, schedule and track maintenance tasks, store warranties and receipts, and manage home projects.
  • Personalize recommendations — suggest relevant maintenance tasks and schedules based on the home details you enter (such as home type, features and, if you choose, your address) and your lifestyle preferences. HomeFitly does not use your device's location.
  • Show reminders — task reminders and other reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device as local notifications. HomeFitly does not send push notifications from a server.
  • Enable family collaboration — allow Premium household members to share tasks, view assignments, and track household activity.
  • Sync data across devices — for Premium members, synchronize your data securely so you can access it across your devices, on iPhone and Android.
  • Verify memberships — confirm your subscription with Apple or Google, or a complimentary membership, so Premium works on every device where you sign in to the same HomeFitly account.
  • Request quotes — show your request to the pros you send it to, deliver their replies to you, limit abuse (we keep a one-way code of the connecting IP address, or your account ID, and a count for a short time), and keep your first request free.
  • Provide customer support — respond to your inquiries and resolve issues when you contact us.
  • Send a requested launch notice — use your website waitlist email and platform choice to notify you when HomeFitly launches for that platform.
  • Process authentication — verify your identity when you sign in and facilitate password resets.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. How We Store Your Data

On-Device Storage

HomeFitly stores your data locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This means your core data remains on your phone or tablet and is protected by your device's built-in security features, including encryption at rest.

Cloud Storage

We use Supabase as our cloud backend for authentication and data synchronization. Supabase infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). When you create an account or use Premium sync features, your data is transmitted to and stored on Supabase servers.

Premium members who use sync have their data stored both locally on each device and in the Supabase cloud backend. Free-tier users primarily rely on local, on-device storage with Supabase used for authentication.

Connected family devices receive only their restricted task and household-activity view, rather than the parent’s full synced data. Their credential is kept in the device’s protected credential storage. The cached shared view expires within 24 hours, or earlier when its access or store-check limits require it. It is hidden when the connection cannot be confirmed and cleared when the device disconnects or learns that access was revoked. Revocation cannot remotely erase a device while it is offline (Section 12).

8. Data Retention

Except for the connected-family, sample-review and existing child-record schedules in Section 12, app-store age-signal records, quote-request periods and nearby-search usage records below, HomeFitly retains your account data while your account is active and you choose to keep it.

  • Individual items: You can delete any individual task, warranty, receipt, project, contact, or other record at any time from within the app. Deleted items are removed from local storage and, if applicable, from the cloud backend.
  • Account data: account records, including subscription and complimentary-membership records, are retained until account deletion, subject to the purpose-specific schedules below and in Section 12. Account deletion removes cloud records and the data on the device used to delete, with the limited exceptions in Section 11. Connected family-device, consent and reusable-parent-proof records are removed with their owner account; separate legacy child-consent and store-withdrawal records have the limited schedules stated below.
  • Quote requests: each pro's link works for 30 days from when it was created or last replaced, unless you close the request sooner. A request, with its photos, the pros' replies and PDF estimates, becomes due for deletion from our servers as soon as you cancel it or delete its project, or 90 days after you close it or its links expire. That includes a request whose links you created but never shared: its links still expire after 30 days, and it's due for deletion 90 days later. A draft you never turned into a request (you didn't create its links) becomes due 30 days after you last changed it. An automatic cleanup runs every day and removes what's due; if there's a lot to remove, or the cleanup is briefly unavailable, it continues on the following days until everything due is gone. Deleting your account deletes them right away. Quotes you've received stay in your project until you delete them. The links saved on your phone are stored only on that phone; they stop working when the request closes or expires, and they're removed from your phone when the request ends (the next time HomeFitly refreshes your requests) or when you delete your account. Short-term counts we use to limit abuse are kept for about two days and then removed by the same daily cleanup.
  • Android nearby-search usage records: protected allowance records keep account and project references, the operation type, time and outcome, a one-way request fingerprint and any relevant business or recipient reference. They do not store the search text or returned business details. Temporary result references expire after 15 minutes; usage records become due for deletion after 62 days. An hourly cleanup removes due rows in batches and continues if there is a backlog. Deleting your account removes its admission and temporary selections and clears the account, project, request and business links from usage records; unlinked counts remain until that cleanup. Your nearby-search access setting and its expiry remain with your account until removed or the account is deleted.
  • Authentication records: Basic authentication logs maintained by Supabase are subject to Supabase's own data retention policies.
  • Connected family information and consent records: Section 12 lists each purpose and retention period, including the difference between routine cleanup and a parent’s explicit deletion request. Minimal deletion-prevention references contain no task or activity contents and remain only while the managed profile exists.
  • App store age signals and withdrawals for signed-in accounts: the latest age-range answer from each of your installations is kept while your account exists and deleted with it; answers that are no longer used (more than 90 days old) are deleted. If Apple or Google tells us that a parent withdrew approval for HomeFitly, we keep a minimal record of that withdrawal, linked to the store's reference for that installation of the app, not to your name or email. An account is linked to an installation when it's signed in there and the app reports the app store's answer to us, and it stays linked until that account is deleted; signing out or not using the app doesn't end the link. We keep the record while any linked account still exists, then for 12 months after the last linked account is deleted, and in any case for at least 12 months after the withdrawal, so the withdrawal can't be bypassed by deleting and recreating an account. If our support team restores access after confirming that the parent approved again, we keep a reference to how that was confirmed (not the parent's messages or contact details) for 24 months.
  • Connected-device store checks: device-associated age-signal records are removed with the device record. A minimal store-install withdrawal record has a separate security purpose so withdrawn permission cannot be bypassed by reconnecting. It is kept for at least 12 months after withdrawal, and longer while an existing signed-in account remains linked under the preceding schedule. It contains the installation reference, not task contents or the parent’s email.

9. Data Security

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit: Data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS encryption) to protect information in transit.
  • Database security: Our Supabase backend enforces Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Your account can access only its own records and, if you join a household, the shared household information that household's rules allow (such as the household's member list, shared tasks and their completion history). Other users cannot query, view, or modify your personal records, and the app cannot change membership records.
  • Password hashing: Your password is cryptographically hashed before storage. We never store or log plain-text passwords.
  • On-device protection: Local data stored on your device benefits from built-in device encryption and security features.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your HomeFitly account and to keep your device software up to date.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your HomeFitly account and its data at any time, with the limited exceptions below. Account deletion is available directly within the app:

  • iPhone and iPad: open the More tab, tap Settings, then Delete Account in the Account section, and confirm.
  • Android: open the More tab, tap Your account, then Delete account, and confirm.

Upon account deletion:

  • Your personal data stored in the cloud (Supabase) is permanently deleted, including your private files, your membership records, your age-group record, and your quote requests with their photos and the pros' replies and estimates, except as described under "What we keep" below.
  • The app removes your account's data from the device you delete from. Copies on other devices where you're signed in aren't removed by our servers; they may stay on those devices until you sign out of HomeFitly there or remove the app.
  • Your authentication credentials are revoked and deleted.
  • Household owners: If you own a household, deleting your account also deletes that household, its member list and pending invitations, and every task you created, including tasks shared with the household, together with their completion history (including completions other members recorded on those tasks). Completions you recorded on tasks other members created are deleted too. Other members keep their own accounts and the tasks they created themselves.
  • This action is irreversible. We cannot recover your data after deletion.

What we keep:

  • For a previously created, separately signed-in 12-year-old account, a minimal record of verified parent consent is kept for 12 months from when the consent ended, then deleted (Section 12). While the child's account still exists, it stays linked to that account through the parent's device entry; if the parent's account is deleted, it no longer names the parent's account.
  • If a parent withdrew approval through Apple or Google, the minimal withdrawal record described in Section 8 is kept for 12 months after the last account linked to that installation is deleted (and at least 12 months after the withdrawal), then deleted. It doesn't name your account.
  • If you used Android nearby search, unlinked allowance counts remain until the usage-record cleanup described in Section 8. They no longer contain your account, project, request or business references.
  • Encrypted recovery backups made before HomeFitly accepted any 12-year-old's account may still contain data from accounts that existed when they were made. They are kept securely and deleted when they're no longer needed for recovery (Section 8). These backups predate the introduction of 12-year-old accounts.

Connected family devices: use Family > Family devices to review shared information, disconnect a device, withdraw consent or delete shared activity. These rights remain available if Premium or device connections end. Delete shared activity removes all synced completion history and points for the selected profile, including parent-recorded entries, and removes device submissions and connections. It keeps the profile and task definitions. Deleting a device alone does not delete history. A recent activity list is not a complete export; contact support for a fuller review. New connected-device mode does not create a separate child account to delete.

If you are unable to delete your account through the app, you can request deletion by emailing support@homefitly.com from the email address associated with your account. You may also visit our Account Deletion page for additional instructions.

Please note that deleting your HomeFitly account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. You must cancel your subscription separately to avoid further charges: on iPhone or iPad, use your Apple ID account settings; on Android, use the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

12. Children’s Privacy and Connected Family Devices

HomeFitly helps households manage tasks together. A parent can continue to manage profiles and record tasks for family members without giving them a login. The new connected family-device mode is for managed profiles aged 12 to 17. A child in this mode has no separate HomeFitly email/password account and never receives the parent’s account session. Children under 12 cannot use this connection mode or create an account; their parent may manage a profile containing the information the parent chooses to enter.

Availability: connected family devices are not open for new connections yet. The steps and schedules in this section describe the feature when available. Existing local home-management and parent-managed Family features remain. Eligible teens who use a separately signed-in account continue to use that account’s existing controls; connecting a family device neither creates such an account nor automatically changes its privileges at age 13.

Parent permission, verification and household sharing

The household owner selects a managed profile, chooses age 12 or 13 to 17 and reviews the notice and the actual household people before agreeing. We record two affirmative choices: that the owner is the child’s parent or legal guardian and agrees to the described collection and use, and that they agree to the described household sharing. Neither starts selected. We record the notice version, profile, parent, time and chore time zone. The time zone is not GPS information.

Adult verification through Epic’s KWS is a separate step described in Section 5. HomeFitly sends a verification request only after the parent explicitly asks; an accepted email request is not a completed verification. A current production verification for the same confirmed parent email may be reused for another profile only after separate HomeFitly permission for that profile, and only while the proof remains valid and unrevoked. A test verification does not authorize real child access.

Only after the required permission and verification can the parent create a one-use connection code. The child’s device must also pass the applicable app-store age and update-permission checks before shared information is displayed. Store checks restrict access; they do not replace parent consent, prove an exact age or give the device more privileges. Check errors or withdrawn permission hide the shared view until resolved.

The connected device sees tasks assigned to its profile or to everyone and submits completion requests for parent approval. The selected profile’s name, approved task activity and points are visible to the household participants allowed to see Family activity, including other adults and children the parent allows into the household. People later added to the household can see the same shared activity. This is not public posting and is not shared with contractors. Other household members do not become the child’s guardian.

This mode cannot manage accounts or membership, purchase, use contacts, contractors or maps, or add notes, photos or messages. It receives a limited view, not the parent’s general account records. HomeFitly does not use this information for advertising. Hosting and app-store services process the information described in Sections 2, 4 and 5; using a restricted connection does not mean that no information leaves the device.

Review, withdraw permission and delete

In Family > Family devices, the parent can use Review shared information, disconnect one device, Withdraw consent and disconnect all, or Delete shared activity. These controls remain available after Premium or new-device access ends, including for an inactive profile with existing consent. The entry may then say Manage existing family devices. On Android, the review and withdrawal buttons are called Review shared data and Stop device sharing. The recent activity list is not a complete export; contact support@homefitly.com from the parent account’s email for a fuller review or help. We check parental authority without requiring unnecessary information.

Disconnecting blocks further requests from that device; withdrawing consent ends all connected access for that profile. Neither alone deletes previous history. Delete shared activity, after a separate confirmation, removes all synced completion history and points for that profile, including entries the parent recorded, removes device submissions and disconnects devices. The parent-created profile and task definitions remain. Minimal consent/security records follow the schedules below.

Server access ends immediately when revoked. A device already offline cannot be remotely erased until it reconnects. Its cached shared view expires within 24 hours, or earlier when its authorization limits require it, and you can clear HomeFitly’s data on that device. Old synced copies are prevented from restoring deleted shared activity.
