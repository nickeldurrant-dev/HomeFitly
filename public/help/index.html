Getting Started

New to HomeFitly? Here's how to get up and running:

  • Continue as a guest to keep home records on this device, or create an account using your email address or sign in with Apple.
  • Set up your home profile by entering your address, home type, size, and key features (e.g., pool, garden, HVAC system).
  • Get personalized tasks — HomeFitly automatically generates a maintenance schedule tailored to your home and lifestyle.
  • Browse the Task Catalog to add additional tasks that matter to you.
Account & Login
  • Signing in: Choose Sign In on the welcome screen. From guest mode, open More > Settings > Account > Sign In, then choose Sign In on the welcome screen. Use the email and password you registered with, or Sign in with Apple if that's how you created your account.
  • Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen. We'll send a reset link to your email.
  • Managing your account: Open More > Settings > Account to view your signed-in account, sign out, or delete your account.
Home Profile

Your home profile helps HomeFitly deliver the right maintenance tasks at the right time.

  • Address: Add or update your home address during onboarding or in Settings.
  • Home details: Specify your home type (house, apartment, condo, etc.), square footage, year built, and number of bedrooms/bathrooms.
  • Features: Tell us about features like a pool, garden, fireplace, or specific HVAC systems so we can recommend relevant tasks.
  • Household info: Add household members to enable family task management.
Tasks & Maintenance
  • Creating tasks: Tap the "+" button on the Tasks screen or browse the Task Catalog to add pre-built maintenance tasks.
  • Reminders: Each task can have a due date and notification reminder so you never miss a maintenance window.
  • Recurring schedules: Set tasks to repeat daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
  • Completing tasks: Mark a task as complete and optionally add notes, photos, or cost information for your records.
  • Task history: View your completion history to track your home maintenance over time.
Projects, Quotes & Contractors
  • Managing projects: Create home improvement projects to organize larger jobs like kitchen remodels, roof repairs, or landscaping.
  • Adding quotes: Attach contractor quotes to any project so you can keep everything in one place.
  • Comparing bids: Use the quote comparison view to evaluate contractors side-by-side on price, timeline, and scope.
  • Tracking progress: Move projects through stages (idea, planning, in-progress, completed) and add photos to document your progress.
  • Find someone nearby on iPhone or iPad: In a version with nearby-business search, open Projects, select a project in Planning and choose Find contractors & request quotes (or Request more quotes if it already has quotes). If the project is an idea, move it to Planning first. In Find someone nearby, tap the map search button. Enter a trade or business and a city or ZIP, then tap Search. You can move the map and tap Search this area. Choose a pin or list result, tap Use selected business, review the details and tap Add contractor. This uses Apple Maps and does not request your device’s location.
  • Android nearby-search availability: new access to in-app Google business searching is not open yet. Manual contacts, saved contacts and the external Maps handoff remain available. The following Google search steps apply only when the feature is enabled for your eligible adult account.
  • Find someone nearby on Android: In versions with nearby search enabled for your adult account, open a project's Request quotes flow and choose Find someone nearby. Enter a trade or business and a city or ZIP, then tap Search businesses. You can move the map and tap Search this area. Choose a pin or list result, tap Use selected business, review the available details, then tap Add contractor.
  • Review before sending: Google may supply a business phone or website, but not an email address. Missing details stay blank; a phone number may not accept texts. HomeFitly creates a separate private link for each contractor and sends no messages automatically. Manual entry, saved contacts and external Maps remain available.
  • Nearby beta allowance: Deliberate business searches and contact lookups use the allowance. Panning or selecting a pin does not make a Places lookup, although the map itself connects to Google. A lookup already sent may count even if it cannot finish. Later, View saved business lets you request current details again; HomeFitly saves the business reference, not a copy of Google's contact listing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
Receipts & Documents
  • Scanning receipts: Use the built-in scanner to photograph and store receipts for home purchases and repairs.
  • Warranty documents: Attach warranty paperwork to keep all your coverage details organized.
  • Home records: Store important home documents like inspection reports, insurance documents, and manuals in one convenient location.
Family Management
  • Setting up a household: Create a household, add profiles you manage and use household invitations for eligible people with their own accounts. A household invitation is different from the restricted family-device connection code described below. Do not give a child your account password.
  • Assigning tasks: Assign tasks to specific family members so everyone knows their responsibilities.
  • Task rotation: Set up automatic task rotation so chores are shared fairly among household members.
  • Kid task approval (Premium): Enable approval mode for younger family members — their completed tasks come to you for review before being marked done.

Connected family devices

New family connections: this mode is not open for new connections yet. The following steps apply when Family devices is available for your account. Your existing parent-managed profiles, assignments and task history remain available.

  1. From Family > Family devices, select the profile you manage and its age group, 12 or 13–17. Review the household people who can see shared activity, the privacy notice and the chore time zone. The time zone comes from your device setting, not GPS.
  2. Confirm that you are the child’s parent or legal guardian and separately agree to sharing their approved task activity and points within this household. Tap Save my consent on iPhone or iPad, or Record my consent on Android. The choices start unchecked.
  3. If verification is needed, tap Request parent verification on iPhone or iPad, or Send parent verification on Android, to request one email to your confirmed account address, then complete Epic’s Kids Web Services verification. Sending the email does not finish verification. An eligible current verification can cover another profile only after you give separate permission for that profile.
  4. Once ready, choose Create a connection code on iPhone or iPad, or Create a device code on Android. On the other device choose Join with a family code (iPhone/iPad) or Join family with a code (Android) and enter the code. A code can be used once and expires after 10 minutes. This creates a restricted connection, not another account; your child needs no email address or password and does not receive your account session.
  5. The connected device must complete its applicable app-store age and update-permission checks before it shows shared information. Follow any displayed parent-approval request. If HomeFitly reports that a check needs approval, cannot be completed, or permission was withdrawn, shared information stays hidden until resolved.
  • What the connected device can do: view tasks assigned to its profile or to everyone, submit completion requests for your approval, and view the household’s shared approved activity and points. It cannot manage accounts or membership, purchase, use contacts, contractors or maps, or add notes, photos or messages.
  • Who can see progress: the household people allowed to see Family activity, including other adults or children you allow to join, can see the profile’s shared approved activity and points. People you add later can see it too. It is not public and is not shared with contractors. Other household members do not become the child’s guardian.
  • Stop a connection: in Family > Family devices, disconnect a single device, or choose Withdraw consent and disconnect all (iPhone/iPad) or Stop device sharing (Android) to end this profile’s connected access. Disconnecting alone does not delete previous activity.
  • Review or delete: choose Review shared information (iPhone/iPad) or Review shared data (Android). Both platforms also offer Delete shared activity. Deleting removes all synced completion history and points for this profile, including entries you recorded yourself, removes device submissions and disconnects devices. The profile and task definitions remain. Read the confirmation before continuing.
  • Rights after access ends: existing controls remain available if Premium or new connections are unavailable; the Family entry may say Manage existing family devices. The recent activity list is not a full export. For a fuller review or help, email support@homefitly.com from the parent account’s email.
  • Offline devices and retention: stopping access blocks new server requests immediately, but a disconnected device cannot be erased remotely until it reconnects. Its cached snapshot expires within 24 hours; you can also clear HomeFitly’s data on that device. See the family-data retention schedule for activity and consent records.

Labelled sample household for app review

If HomeFitly has explicitly supplied an account with sample review access, the screen says Sample household for review. Its people, chores and points are fictional. Read the sample explanation and choose I understand I am using fictional sample data before continuing. This acknowledgement does not verify parenthood or give permission for a real child. Applicable app-store checks still apply to the connected device.

Use only the supplied sample connection instructions. Sample access ends at the time shown and supports at most two devices over its lifetime; reconnecting does not extend it. Delete sample activity clears the demonstration’s activity and disconnects its devices, while real household history stays unchanged. It does not delete the reviewer’s account. See sample review data and retention. Sample access is separate from opening new family connections to customers.

Warranties
  • Adding warranties: Store warranty details for appliances, systems, and home components including purchase date, coverage period, and provider information.
  • Expiration tracking: HomeFitly tracks all your warranty expiration dates in one place so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Warranty alerts: Receive notifications before a warranty expires so you can take action — whether that means filing a claim or renewing coverage.

Receipt scanning, warranty alerts and quote comparison are also available within the free plan's record limits.

Pricing: $4.99/month or $49.99/year (save over 15% with annual billing).

Open More > Settings and tap your Free Plan or HomeFitly Premium card to open Membership. Choose See Premium plans when available and review the current price and billing period in the app-store plan picker.

Restore Purchases

If you've previously purchased a Premium subscription and need to restore it on a new device or after reinstalling the app:

  • Open HomeFitly and sign in with the same HomeFitly account used for your subscription.
  • Open More > Settings, tap your plan card to open Membership, then choose Restore Purchases.
  • HomeFitly checks the store purchase and your membership status. Follow the result shown in the app; if the check cannot finish, use the displayed retry option or contact support.

Use the Apple ID or Google account that owns the subscription as well as the matching HomeFitly account. Complimentary and household access are shown separately from store subscriptions.

Manage or Cancel Subscription

Your HomeFitly Premium subscription is managed through the app store you subscribed with. You can manage or cancel it in two ways:

  • On iPhone/iPad: Open the Settings app > tap your Apple ID (your name at the top) > Subscriptions > select HomeFitly to manage or cancel.
  • On Android: Open the Google Play Store app > tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > select HomeFitly to manage or cancel.
  • From the HomeFitly app: Open More > Settings and tap your plan card to open Membership. Apple-billed plans show Manage Apple Subscription when available. For Google Play billing, use Google Play's subscription settings.

If you cancel, your Premium features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Reporting a Problem

Found a bug or experiencing an issue? We'd love to help.

  • Send an email to support@homefitly.com describing the problem.
  • Please include your device model, operating system version, and HomeFitly app version (found in Settings) to help us investigate faster.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful if the issue is visual.

We take every report seriously and will work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

  • From the app: Go to More > Settings > Account > Delete Account. Follow the confirmation prompts to permanently delete your account.
  • This action removes all your data including your home profile, tasks, projects, receipts, and household information.
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

For more information, visit our Account Deletion page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is HomeFitly free to use?
Yes! The free plan includes unlimited tasks, recurring schedules, task history and recommendations, a home profile, reminders, and two starter checklists, plus up to 3 warranty records, 5 service contacts, 1 home project, 10 receipts, and 3 project ideas. Receipt scanning, warranty alerts and quote comparison are also available within these record limits. Premium adds cloud sync, more checklists, unlimited saved records and household features.
What's included in Premium?
Premium adds cloud sync, all 13 checklists, unlimited projects, warranties, contacts, receipts and ideas, family household management with task assignment and rotation, kid task approval workflows, and priority support. Tasks, receipt scanning, warranty alerts, quote comparison and recommendations are also available free within the free plan's record limits. It's available for $4.99/month or $49.99/year; check the app-store plan picker for the current price and billing period.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel through the app store you subscribed with. On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, then go to Subscriptions and select HomeFitly. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, then go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions and select HomeFitly. In HomeFitly, open More > Settings and tap your plan card to open Membership. Apple-billed plans show Manage Apple Subscription when available; Google Play subscriptions are managed in Google Play. Your Premium access will continue until the end of your current billing period.
Can I use HomeFitly on multiple devices?
Yes. On your own devices, sign in with the same HomeFitly account. For a child’s restricted device, use the family connection flow when available instead of sharing your password. Premium includes cloud sync when you are signed in and connected; More > Settings > Data & Backup shows the current sync status. To check a store subscription, open your plan card in Settings and use Restore Purchases on Membership with the store account that owns the subscription. Follow the displayed result if it cannot finish.
How do I delete my account and data?
You can delete your account from within the app by navigating to More > Settings > Account > Delete Account. This removes your account and its cloud records, subject to the limited security and recovery exceptions in our Privacy Policy. Deleting an account cannot be undone and does not cancel a store subscription. You can separately review or delete a connected profile’s shared activity in Family > Family devices. For more details, visit our Account Deletion page.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. HomeFitly takes your privacy and data security seriously. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Your personal information is never sold to third parties. For full details, please review our Privacy Policy.
